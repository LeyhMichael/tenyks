(Gen)AI offers in brief

The frameworks that come up on almost every request. Summaries here, full decks linked below.

Deploy

AI in everyday tasks

Inject tools (Copilot, ChatGPT) into existing workflows without changing the operating model. Broad productivity of 10 to 20%.

Reshape

Rethink functions and workflows

Rewire core functions and how people work. Radical productivity and speed gains of 30 to 50%.

Invent

New business models

Create new revenue through AI-powered services, monetizing assets and data.

Leading companies focus about 80% of AI investment on Reshape and Invent. The E2E transformation runs in three waves: Accelerate, Scale, Embed.
10%AI Application

Copilots and agents in high-value workflows.

20%Data and Technology

Modern data foundation and scalable AI platforms.

70%People, Org, Process

Redesign processes, roles, skills, and governance. The POP focus.

Three complementary career paths

Client Focus (CF)

Case-intensive delivery

The most case-intensive path. You embed in case teams and deliver expertise, analysis, and assets directly through staffing and workshops.

Topic Activation (TA)

Player-coach model

A player-coach path. You combine expert advisory, offer and asset development, and selective staffing to activate a topic for your practice and its case teams.

Research

Data into insight

You source, validate, and synthesize external data and information, turning it into insight for case and proposal teams.

Key case terminology

OPPL

The management team: Officers (MDPs), Partners, Principals, Project Leaders.

XO, Executing Officer

The MDP who is the day-to-day point of contact for the case.

PL, Project Leader

Leads the day-to-day case team and the delivery of its modules.

MDP

Managing Director and Partner. BCG's partner level; the Officers on a case.

Module

A concrete unit of work that helps solve the case.

Working Team

Associates, consultants, and others doing the day-to-day work.

PA, Practice Area

The topic or sector group that owns offers and experts. Vantage sits within PAs.

SBU

Specialty Business Unit. Vantage is one of several SBUs supporting cases.

GRO

Global Research Organization. BCG's dedicated research arm; some teammates sit here.

Expert Advisory checklist

Understanding

Understand the context and challenge the team where useful. Know not just what they ask, but why.

Expectations and prioritization

Set expectations on what you can deliver. Communicate timeline and pricing if more than roughly 8 hours of work.

Connections, transparency, and answering

Make sure you actually answer the question, frame the larger context, and annotate your sources. Be clear on limitations, but never write "this may help" and leave them to figure it out. "I don't have the answer" full stop is never an acceptable response. If you can't answer directly, always redirect to the right team, share key contacts, offer to help build the PoV or perspective, or offer to do the research.

Pushing deeper

Shift requests from advisory to deep staffing where it fits. Offer next steps and follow up.

6 Billability

Vantage is a billable business. C-billability is the key metric for profitable impact.

C-billability targets

45%PA / Geo portfolio
>40%TA
55%CF
50%Research (PA / Geo)
50-55%GRO
Topic activation. Our PA target for Topic Activation is 45%.

C-billability and CD-billability

CD-billability = C-billability + Client Investment + Innovation Fund
  • Charge Innovation Fund project codes for Expert Advisory work or when staffed on those projects.
  • For client work linked to an Innovation Fund, charge the linked C-code, not the Innovation Fund code. The internal "600" code does not provide client billability.
  • Avoid "Other Time Tracking" codes. They do not count toward billability.
  • Verify the code type at projectlookup.bcg.com.

7 Timesheet

Find and verify your codes, then track and submit your time each week in the Vantage Console.

1. Find and verify codes

Use projectlookup.bcg.com to verify a code's accuracy and type. Use Knowledge Navigator profiles, under Cases, to find codes by requestor.

2. Track and submit in the Console

The timesheet is done in the Vantage Console (ServiceNow). Add a timesheet entry per engagement, enter time per code, and set the status to delivered.

Vantage Console

3. Check the dashboard

After submitting, confirm your timesheet went through correctly and that your billability looks healthy on the Vantage Billability dashboard.
