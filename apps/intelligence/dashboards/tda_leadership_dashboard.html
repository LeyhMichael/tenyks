The Five Buckets — What They Cover
AI Ambition & Strategy
Setting the AI vision: articulating ambition, defining value pools, and selecting priority opportunities. Includes all Build for the Future (BFF) requests as the flagship strategic engagement vehicle.
What's included
Build for the Future (BFF) decks and inquiries · AI / digital maturity assessments, readiness diagnostics and the Digital Acceleration Index · AI strategy, vision and roadmap requests, ambition and investment cases.
Sub-buckets
Build for the Future (BFF)Maturity Assessment & DiagnosticsAI Strategy, Vision & Roadmap
Enterprise Value Assurance
Orchestrating delivery and value: governance and programme management, value tracking and resource management, delivery model and ecosystem partnerships.
What's included
Value tracking, impact and forecasting (ROI, KPI, demand planning, cost) · programme and delivery management (TMO / PMO, delivery model, portfolio) · ecosystems and partnerships (vendors, alliances, build-vs-buy).
Sub-buckets
Value Tracking, Impact & ForecastingEcosystems & PartnershipsProgram & Delivery Management
Enterprise Enablement
Making AI stick across the organisation: AI-first org and operating model, workforce reinvention, and leadership / culture activation.
What's included
Operating model and org design (AI-first org, CoE, process/workflow redesign) · workforce, talent and upskilling (productivity, academies) · adoption, change management and culture / literacy programmes.
Sub-buckets
Operating Model & Org DesignWorkforce, Talent & UpskillingAdoption, Change & Culture
Enterprise Foundations
The tech and data backbone for AI at scale: platforms, data foundations, and the Risk & Responsible AI layer (cyber, evals, regulatory compliance).
What's included
Agentic frameworks and orchestration (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen) and AI/tech platform selection, MLOps, RAG and architecture · data foundations, fabric and governance · Risk & Responsible AI (guardrails, cyber, EU AI Act, GDPR) · digital transformation and legacy IT modernisation.
Sub-buckets
Agentic & AI Platform / Tech StackData FoundationsRisk & Responsible AIDigital Transformation & IT Modernisation
AI Applications & Use Cases
Demand for AI applications across business functions and industries — Gen AI / LLM use cases, agentic solutions, and the long tail of credentials, proposals, and case examples requested by case teams.
What's included
Agentic use cases and solutions — agents, copilots, assistants and automation · Function use cases — AI applied to business functions (sales, marketing, HR, finance, procurement, operations, customer service) · Industry use cases — Gen AI demand cut by sector (banking, retail, telco, healthcare, energy, public sector, etc.).
Sub-buckets

Requests that don't fit any of the five strategic layers. Mostly low-information records — empty or single-character titles, generic administrative labels ('AD HOC', 'request', staffing-role names), and substantive but goal-less asks. Signal for data-capture quality at intake.
What's included
Title-less or single-character records · generic admin labels (AD HOC, request, holiday) · staffing-role labels (Consultant, Partner, BCG Vantage Analyst) · substantive but goal-less asks where the requester didn't specify the intent.

Illustrative — the three highest-potential asset gaps surfaced by a deep-dive on the request corpus. For discussion, not a committed roadmap.
OPPORTUNITY 1
Agent Productionization EconomicsLead rec
114 requests +260% YoY (Q1)
Example request
"Agentic AI in Biopharma Operations — capabilities required (people, data, tech, process) … we are supporting UCB TechOps through a transformation." Desk · Vantage
Closest material today
Building Effective Enterprise Agents & the agentic platform / ecosystem primers — all architecture-led. None covers the economics or POC→production sequencing teams keep asking for.
Proposed asset to close the gap
Agent Productionization Pack
  • Cost-to-productionize driver tree
  • Scaling / ROI estimator (tool)
  • Deployed-agent credentials library with quantified impact
OPPORTUNITY 2
Chief AI Officer Launch Pad
51 requests dormant → 15 (Q1-26)
Example request
"We are preparing materials for a pharma client ahead of a meeting with their new CAIO this Thursday." Desk · Vantage — "Request for AI operating model materials"
Closest material today
AI-First Organization covers operating-model / org design, but there is no dedicated CAIO asset — no mandate, first-100-days, or "when to appoint" benchmark.
Proposed asset to close the gap
CAIO Launch Pad
  • Mandate & first-100-days playbook + org-placement guide
  • Board / C-suite AI-narrative templates
  • "When to appoint a CAIO" maturity benchmark
OPPORTUNITY 3
AI / GenAI Spend & Investment Benchmarks
65 requests +133% YoY (Q1)
Example request
"AI Investments — IT budget Benchmark / Trends … the '1.7% of revenue on AI' you mention comes from the AI Radar — do we have more?" Desk · Vantage
Closest material today
Only scattered one-off datapoints (AI Radar headline %, BFF AI-budget charts). No maintained spend-benchmark dataset — and demand is perishable and partly investor / PE-diligence driven.
Proposed asset to close the gap
AI Investment Benchmark
  • Maintained spend dataset (% revenue / % IT, by sector + geography)
  • GenAI allocation view (share to agents / infra)
  • AI investment calculator (tool)
Fund it (3) → lead it (2) → build & scale it (1). #3 and #2 are C-suite door-openers; #1 is the high-volume, fast-growing practitioner workhorse — if you build one first, build #1.
