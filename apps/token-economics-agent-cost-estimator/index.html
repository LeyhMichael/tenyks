Methodology & Sources: Architecture framework from BCG AI Platforms Group, "Building Effective Enterprise AI Agents" (Tom Martin, David Heurtaux, et al., Nov 2025). Token consumption benchmarks: Gartner AI Infrastructure Report Mar 2026 (5–30× multiplier vs standard GenAI), BCG case experience. LLM pricing as of May 2026: pricepertoken.com · costgoat.com. People cost benchmarks: Glassdoor/LinkedIn 2026 + 30% loaded cost premium. Infrastructure costs: vendor public pricing pages. Disclaimer: This is a directional cost model for strategic planning. Actual costs depend on use-case specifics, vendor negotiations, and operational decisions. Pricing data last validated May 2026 and will evolve over time.

Scope: Models technology & people cost (the 30% in BCG's 10/20/70 model); business-change delivery cost (the 70%) is excluded. The Value & Benefits module estimates the benefit side using Gartner's value methodology (headcount shift + productivity uplift, net of productivity leak) to produce ROI, Agent Value Multiple (AVM) and payback. Value is directional and depends heavily on whether productivity gains are actively harvested.
